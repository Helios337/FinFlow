# Business Requirements - FinanceOps360

## Business users
- Finance Manager (daily oversight of payables/receivables)
- Accounts Payable / AR clerks (discrepancy resolution)
- Supply-chain / operations lead (inventory health)
- Auditors (controls evidence)

## Business problems
MedTech Dynamics Pvt. Ltd. (fictional) runs AP/AR, bank rec, and distributed
inventory across 8 warehouses and multiple currencies. Volume makes manual
spreadsheets error-prone. The system must:
1. Reconcile invoice ↔ payment ↔ bank automatically.
2. Detect duplicates, mismatches, missing and anomalous transactions.
3. Enforce financial controls (PO match, auth limits, inventory identity).
4. Expose KPIs to finance and operations via dashboards.
5. Let non-technical users ask business questions in natural language.

## Data sources
Synthetic CSVs (deterministic, seeded) generated by `python/data_generator.py`:
vendors, customers, products, warehouses, employees, POs, PO items, vendor &
customer invoices, payments, bank transactions, inventory, inventory movements,
expenses, FX rates.

## KPIs
Total payables / receivables (INR), outstanding & overdue invoices, reconciliation
rate, mismatch value, inventory value, low/over-stock SKUs, control-violation count,
anomaly count, on-time payment %, avg payment delay.

## Controls
C1 duplicate invoice, C2 payment authorization, C3 invoice ≤ PO, C4 payment ≤
invoice, C5 inventory identity, C6 late payment, C7 wrong bank counterparty,
C8 duplicate bank transaction.

## Expected outputs
- PostgreSQL database + 40+ SQL queries / views.
- Reconciliation & controls reports (CSV + DB).
- Excel reconciliation workbook (8 sheets).
- Power BI model + 4 dashboards (publish to Service).
- FastAPI + AI copilot (read-only, validated SQL).

## Assumptions
- Reporting currency INR; FX via daily `fx_rates`.
- Bank ledger signed (debit negative).
- 1 invoice ↔ 1 payment (partial payments allowed).
- Synthetic data only; no real confidential data.
